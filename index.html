About

Hi, I'm Diego.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science & Informatics at Emory University, where I started in Fall 2025. Before that I earned my B.S. in Informatics (with a minor in Information & Computer Sciences) at UC Irvine, working with Armando Beltran, Franceli Cibrian, and Gillian Hayes.

Research. I study how people make sense of data across different senses — visual, tactile, and tabular encodings — and how to make those representations more accessible. Lately I've been developing codebooks for a user study on perceptual accuracy, and running a matched-design study that extends prior work on visual and tactile encodings.

Before Emory. At UC Irvine I analyzed qualitative data from smartwatch studies and built Streamlit dashboards to visualize over a hundred distinct themes. During a summer at UC Berkeley, I built an information-retrieval and semantic-clustering system over ~490 legal documents (32,849 pages), with React interfaces to surface analytics for journalists and legal teams.
