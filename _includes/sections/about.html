Hello and welcome

My name is Diego Guzman and I am a first-year Computer Science and Informatics Ph.D. student at Emory University where I am fortunate to be supported as a NIH NIGMS IMSD Fellow. Before joining Emory, I earned my Bachelor of Science in Informatics with a specialization in Human-Computer Interaction and a minor in Information and Computer Sciences from UC Irvine. Here I had the opportunity to work at the Social & Technological Action Research (STAR) Lab with Dr. Jesus Armando Beltran Verdugo, Dr. Franceli Cibrian, and Dr. Gillian Hayes.

Broadly speaking, I am interested in using Human-Computer Interaction, Ubiquitous Computing, and Machine Learning to enhance human agency for individuals with chronic health conditions and physical impairments.
